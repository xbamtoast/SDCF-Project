Hope Grant Application

Information Section

Silver Dollar City Foundation is a private 501c3 organization and the philanthropic arm of the 1880s theme park Silver Dollar City. Funding for the foundation is made possible through the generosity of Silver Dollar City, Showboat Branson Belle, White Water and other generous donors. Our mission is to “partner with communities to equip and inspire children,” and our vision is “today’s children become tomorrow’s thriving adults.” We work to fulfill our mission and vision by partnering with 14 local school districts to provide each of those districts with funding to meet both immediate physical needs as well as make long-term impacts.

Through our partnership with Care to Learn, our Together for Kids funding helps you meet the immediate physical needs of students, whether that is clothing and shoes to eyeglasses to even helping parents with rent or utilities. Our Hope Grants are focused on future, long-term impacts on families and children in our community and can be a great catalyst for a bigger project. The amount of funding available for Hope Grants is based on what the foundation has available to award, which means award amounts may vary year to year and future funding is not always guaranteed.

As you complete this year’s Hope Grant application, I encourage you think about how your proposed project will bring hope for a brighter future for the children and families you serve. What are the biggest issues facing your students and families in your district and can you begin to address one of those issues with the Hope Grant? Think of the Hope Grant as a jump-start grant for a project or program that without these funds would not come to fruition.

Things to know about applying:

  • Application Deadline: August 14th, 2026
  • Maximum Award Amount for 2026-2027 School Year: $10,000 per district
